# Digital Staff Roster

The Railsstarter Engineering Wiki is operated by a standard digital staff team of seven roles. The
human owner talks to the Chief of Staff, who routes work to the six domain agents. Each role owns a
set of wiki categories and has a dedicated page under `wiki/staff/`.

## Roles and ownership

| Role | ID | Owned categories | Sensitivity | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chief of Staff | `chief` | projects, decisions, playbooks, open-questions | confidential | Cross-functional coordination; routes work to the other roles. |
| Sales | `sally` | sales | internal | Pipeline, deals, prospects, and sales process. |
| Marketing | `mark` | marketing | internal | Positioning, campaigns, content, and channels. |
| Finance | `felix` | finance | confidential | Budgets, costs, forecasts, and financial reporting. |
| Customer Success | `casey` | customers | internal | Customer accounts, onboarding, support history, retention. |
| People | `parker` | people | confidential | Team, roles, hiring, and people operations. |
| Legal & Compliance | `lex` | legal | confidential | Contracts, compliance, policies, and legal review. |

## Routing model

- The Chief of Staff is the single point of contact for the human owner.
- Each domain agent owns its categories and is the authority for knowledge in that domain.
- This roster is the starting team; roles may be added, removed, or renamed in
  `wiki/lisa-wiki.config.json` as the organization evolves.
